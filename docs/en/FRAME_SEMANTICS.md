# Frames (Frame Semantics / Fillmore) in Emotalk

**Language / Idioma:** [English](README.html) | [Português (Brasil)](../pt-br/FRAME_SEMANTICS.html)

Emotalk includes a **Frames** feature inspired by **Frame Semantics** (Charles J. Fillmore) and the **FrameNet** project: semantic frames with multiple roles (frame elements) that the user fills with vocabulary words. The app speaks the full sentence.

## What is Frame Semantics (Fillmore)

In linguistics, **Frame Semantics** describes word meaning in terms of **semantic frames**: situation types with participants and roles (frame elements). For example, the Giving frame involves who gives (Donor), what is given (Theme), and to whom (Recipient). The verb “give” evokes this frame. **FrameNet** (UC Berkeley) is a lexical database that maps words to frames and annotates sentences with filled roles.

In Emotalk, Frames let you build full sentences by choosing a frame (e.g. Giving, Eating, Going, Wanting) and filling each role with a compatible word (filtered by semantic type: Who, What, Where, How).

## How to enable and use

1. **Enable:** Settings → **Fillmore frames** → check «Enable Frames (Frame Semantics / Fillmore)» → **Save**.
2. The **Frames** button (🖼️) appears in the sidebar (between Sentence frames and Settings).
3. Tap **Frames** → choose a frame from the list (Giving, Eating, Going, Wanting, Being, Seeing).
4. For each role in the frame, choose a word from the grid (e.g. «Donor» → people; «Thing given» → objects; «Recipient» → people).
5. The preview shows the sentence (e.g. «Mum gives apple to John»). Tap **Speak sentence** to vocalise and add to history.

## Available frames

| Frame | Roles (frame elements) | Example sentence |
|-------|-------------------------|-------------------|
| **Giving** | Donor, Thing given, Recipient | ___ gives ___ to ___ |
| **Eating** | Eater, Food | ___ eats ___ |
| **Going** | Agent, Goal | ___ goes ___ |
| **Wanting** | Experiencer, Desired | ___ wants ___ |
| **Being (state)** | Entity, State | ___ is ___ |
| **Seeing** | Perceiver, Phenomenon | ___ sees ___ |

Words shown at each step are filtered by semantic role (Who → people categories; What → objects; Where → places; How → describe/emotions), aligned with Colourful Semantics and the board vocabulary.
